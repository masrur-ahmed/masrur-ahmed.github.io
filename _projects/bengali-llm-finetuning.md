Fine-tuned Llama 3.2 3B and Qwen 2.5 7B on Bengali instruction datasets, improving MMMLU scores from **0.36 to 0.74** and **0.68 to 0.81**, respectively.

[View the fine-tuned Llama model on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/masrur/llama3.2-3B-4bit-lora-finetuned-bengali).
