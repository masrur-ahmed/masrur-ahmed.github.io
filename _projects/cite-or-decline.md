Built a chatbot for STEM lecture videos that isolates retrieval to the active course, ranks transcript segments with chapter summaries, and answers with clickable timestamped citations or declines unsupported questions.

In a semester-long deployment on VideoPoints, we evaluated **833 user messages**. **70.5%** of responses included citations, and no cross-course retrieval was observed. On the EduVidQA real-world test split, the system improved correct-lecture retrieval by **6.3 percentage points** over a dense-only baseline.

This work was published as a first-author paper at **EMNLP 2026, Industry Track**.
