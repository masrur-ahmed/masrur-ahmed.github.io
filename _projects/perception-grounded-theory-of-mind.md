I am developing a perception-grounded Theory of Mind framework for robotic reasoning. The framework tracks uncertainty about observed events and separates what is true from what an agent believes.

On **134 held-out cases**, it reduced false suspicion by **12 percentage points** (95% confidence interval: 6–17) compared with an LLM baseline given the same observations.

This research is ongoing.
